Thank you for exploring my portfolio. If you would like to ask about a piece, discuss a commission, or share a creative opportunity, I would be happy to hear from you.

I welcome enquiries from individuals, galleries, local businesses, and other makers. Tell me a little about your idea so we can begin a conversation.

Commission Enquiries

If you are interested in a custom ceramic piece, include a description of what you have in mind. Useful details include the type of object, approximate size, preferred colours, and the number of pieces.

You are welcome to share reference images to help explain your idea. I can then discuss the possibilities, the making process, and a suitable timeline.

Handmade ceramics take time to develop, dry, glaze, and fire. Sharing your preferred completion date early helps with planning.

Collaborations and Opportunities

I am interested in opportunities to work with other artists, designers, and small businesses. This could include a shared collection, an exhibition, or a project that brings ceramics into a different setting.

If you have a collaboration in mind, please explain the project, who is involved, and what you hope to create. I enjoy hearing how another person’s approach might connect with my own.

Questions About a Piece

If you have seen something you like on my Works page, mention the collection name and describe the piece in your email.

I can provide more information about its materials, dimensions, finish, and availability. Since each piece is handmade, there may be small differences between items in the same collection.
